# Watercolor village residents

Approved scope: watercolor NPC artwork and a populated village. Five shopkeepers,
two tea guests, two chatting residents, two walkers and one gardener (12 total).
Retain the tutorial guide at its existing position and interaction.

1. Generate ten transparent character originals with distinct outfits/props;
   reuse tea-guest and chat-resident variants for two residents each. Use Ludo
   for two looping in-place walk spritesheets and save all outputs locally.
2. Add one reusable VillageResident node script for breathing, walk/pause,
   obstacle/player clearance and occasional proximity speech. Place residents
   near shops, plaza, teahouse and garden. Ambient NPCs do not block movement.
3. Verify population, collision-safe routes, pause/resume, unchanged tutorial
   interaction and area transitions in isolated profiles. Inspect headed village
   captures and rerun relevant existing checks. Preserve prompts and evidence.

No new shop inventories, crafting recipes or daily schedule simulation.
